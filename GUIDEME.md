# How to Play Your Infinite Adventure

Welcome, traveler! This guide will help you navigate the endless possibilities of your unique journey.

## Getting Started

### Starting Your Adventure
When you first launch the application, the AI game master will automatically craft the beginning of your story. You'll be presented with:

1.  A **scene-setting image** on the left.
2.  The first part of your **story** below the image.
3.  A set of **choices** to decide your first action.

### Making Choices
Simply read the story segment and click on the button that corresponds to the action you want to take. Once you make a choice, the engine will craft the next part of your unique story, generate a new image, update the story text, and present you with new choices.

## Mastering Your Journey

### The Dynamic Sidebar
On the right side of the screen, you'll find your adventure status, which provides a rich, at-a-glance summary of your journey:

-   **Character Portrait**: At the top of the sidebar, you'll see a portrait that changes to reflect the character you're interacting with or your own character's current emotional state.
-   **Current Quest**: This tells you your main objective. The AI will update this as you complete goals.
-   **Inventory**: This lists all the items you've collected. **Click on any item to view a detailed description**.
-   **Lore Codex**: Entries are automatically added as you discover new characters, locations, and items.
-   **Achievements**: Track your earned accolades here.

### Engaging in Combat
Sometimes, your path will lead to conflict. When a battle begins:
- The game will enter a **Combat Mode**.
- Your choices will change to specific combat actions like "Attack", "Defend", or "Use Item".
- Face unique enemies with varying tactics.
- Once the battle is over, the story returns to normal narrative mode.

### Saving Your Progress
Your progress is now enhanced with **Persistent Cloud Saves**.
-   **Cloud Sync**: Sign in using Supabase Auth (e.g., via Google) to sync your progress across different devices.
-   **Manual Save**: Click the "Save Game" button at any time. You have three slots to save your progress.
-   **Automatic Save**: The game automatically saves your progress every five turns into a dedicated "Autosave" slot.
-   **Local Fallback**: If you are not signed in, the game will automatically use your browser's local storage to keep your progress safe.

## The Living World

### Adaptive Difficulty
The world subtly reacts to your skill. Challenges scale automatically based on your success or failure to keep the adventure engaging.

### Immersive Sounds
Listen for subtle audio cues like clicks, chimes, and triumphant shimmers that enhance your immersion.

## Disclaimer: The Nature of the Infinite
This is not a traditional game. Please keep the following in mind:

-   **Unpredictable AI**: The story and characters are generated by a creative AI. The narrative can be unpredictable and occasionally inconsistent.
-   **Visual Consistency**: The AI strives for visual continuity, but dream-like variations in images may occur.
-   **No True "End"**: This adventure is designed to be endless. Explore, create, and see where your choices take you!
